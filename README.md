## What's here

Most of my professional work lives under NDA inside attractions. This account is where I keep the tools and samples I can share.

### [houdini_swept_path_optimization_tool](https://github.com/camkania/houdini_swept_path_optimization_tool)

Swept path analysis for ride vehicles and moving show elements. Generates the volume a vehicle occupies through its full motion path, then reduces the resulting mesh to something a clearance review can load and interrogate. Because someone had to finally answer: "How do we know this won't hit anything?"

### [blender_projection_mapping_toolkit](https://github.com/camkania/blender_projection_mapping_toolkit)

Tools for authoring mapped media content against real projection geometry. So everyone knows what to expect when the projectors finally turn on. 

### [blender_snapshot_tool](https://github.com/camkania/blender_snapshot_tool)

Snapshots animated objects at each frame across an interval, turning a motion path into static geometry you can see all at once. Useful for reviewing arcs, spacing, and envelopes in a single viewport rather than by scrubbing.

## How I work

**Motion inside real limits.** Animation within physical velocity, acceleration, and jerk constraints. Its got to feel right and its got to be safe. 

**Show systems and media.** Disguise, OmniCal, Winscript, Unreal, TouchDesigner, QLab. Because communication is key and timing is everything.

**Real-time and tooling.** Unreal, Blender, Maya, Houdini. Python, C++, Qt. Digital twins, previz toolkits, LiDAR scan-to-mesh pipelines, synthetic data generation.

**Research.** Physics simulation, spacetime constraints, and procedural systems for animation authoring. Currently working toward publication with Prof. Bo Zhu at Georgia Tech.
